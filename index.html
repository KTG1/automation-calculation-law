Common questions

Understand the estimate.

Explore how the calculator works, what may affect a claim, and what the estimate leaves out.

How is the estimated claim value calculated?

The calculator adds economic damages to an estimate for pain and suffering. The pain-and-suffering estimate applies your selected multiplier to past and future medical expenses.

What does the likely range mean?

The range is 15% below and above the estimated take-home amount. It illustrates uncertainty and is not a prediction or guarantee.

When is the attorney fee deducted?

The calculator applies the entered attorney fee percentage after reducing the gross estimate for your share of fault.

What should I include as case costs?

Examples can include filing fees, medical records, depositions, investigators, and expert witnesses. Fee arrangements vary.

Does this calculator provide legal advice?

No. It is a general planning tool. A qualified attorney can assess deadlines, evidence, insurance coverage, local law, and the facts of a specific matter.

Can comparative fault eliminate recovery?

Rules differ by jurisdiction. Some systems reduce recovery by a percentage, while others can bar recovery at a threshold. The calculator only models a percentage reduction.
